Extra-Curricular Activities: Padhai ke sath-sath yahan sports, music, drama, aur coding clubs jaise dheron options hote hain jo aapke talents ko nikharte hain. Independence aur Personal Growth: College life aapko khud ke faisle lena, time manage karna aur zimmedari uthana sikhati hai, jo school se bilkul alag hai.
